01 / The idea

A handset with a sense of presence.

Kinetix V-01 explores a different kind of personal device: tactile, architectural, and attentive to the way sound and light shape a moment.

The concept is shown from two sides: a wide landscape display and a sculpted rear housing. An electric-blue light line traces the edge; a warm ceramic grip gives the cool metal a counterpoint.

03 / V-01 at a glance

Defined by what you can feel.

A plausible flagship spec direction, developed to make the concept tangible. These are proposed targets, not confirmed or commercially available specifications.

Form
Premium 5G smartphone concept

Designed as a conventional smartphone, with a distinctive acoustic and light-led exterior.

Display
6.4-inch LTPO AMOLED · 1–120 Hz

20:9, 1.5K class (around 2,700 × 1,200), HDR support and peak brightness target around 2,000 nits.

Processor
Current-generation 4 nm flagship-class platform

Targeted for fast everyday performance, on-device AI features and sustained gaming. Final chipset would depend on production timing.

Memory
12 GB RAM · 256 GB storage

UFS 4.0 class storage; no microSD expansion target.

Rear cameras
50 MP main with OIS + 50 MP 2× telephoto

Large-sensor main camera and a portrait-friendly optical zoom camera, aligned with the two prominent rear elements in the concept.

Front camera
32 MP · 4K video target

Center-aligned selfie camera with electronic stabilization.

Battery
4,800 mAh · 50 W wired charging

Up to 15 W Qi2-compatible wireless charging target; real charging times would depend on the final design.

Audio & light
Stereo speakers · spatial lighting strip

Dual speakers, three-microphone noise reduction and a configurable cyan notification light. No separate light-field display is assumed.

Build
Grade 5 titanium · ceramic grip inlays

Concept target: about 8.6 mm thick and 200 g. Weight and dimensions would need engineering validation.

Durability
IP68 water and dust resistance target

Sealing, drop resistance and repairability would need confirmation on a production design.

Connectivity
5G · Wi-Fi 7 · Bluetooth 5.4 · NFC · USB-C

Dual SIM with eSIM support is the proposed configuration; regional band support would vary.

Software
Android-based interface

A clean, long-support software experience is the design goal. Update policy and OS version are not yet defined.

Status
Concept specification

All added performance, camera, battery and connectivity figures are realistic design targets for illustration. They are not verified product specifications, a release promise or an offer for sale.
